## 2. Official 3D XO Bot Mascot

- **File Path:** `branding/xo_mascot.jpg`
- **Character Description:**
  - **Body:** Vibrant lime green with white **"XO"** emblem embossed across the chest.
  - **Arms & Ears:** Smooth cyan-to-blue gradient extremities with friendly waving hand pose.
  - **Face Visor:** Obsidian dark mask screen with expressive, luminous multi-layered eyes (blue, green, black) and friendly smile.
  - **Footwear:** Modern chunky black sneakers with vibrant green and blue accents and green **"x"** badge on the sides.
  - **Backpack:** Dark utility pack with subtle green cross branding.
